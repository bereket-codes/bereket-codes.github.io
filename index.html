About me

Education &
Background

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Unity University (Class of 2026) with a deep focus on frontend development. I build interfaces that are fast, accessible and visually refined — and I care as much about the millisecond a hover takes to settle as about the layout grid.

A great website isn't just about looking good — it's about making the user feel at home instantly.

I care most about the unglamorous parts: validating input before it reaches the screen, handling loading and error states so a page never looks broken, keeping the code small enough to reason about, and not shipping anything that leaks what it shouldn't. Every project here is deployed and clickable — I'd rather ship something small and finished than something large and half-working.
